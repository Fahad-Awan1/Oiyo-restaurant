Good to know

Allergens & dietary care

Every item carries simple labels. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs, so please tell us about any allergy and we will guide you through the menu.

  • V Vegan: no animal products
  • GF Gluten-free ingredients
  • NF Made without nuts
Which milks do you offer?
Whole milk from a family dairy in Tillamook, plus our house oat milk at no extra charge. Almond is available on request (not nut-free).
Is the matcha sweetened?
Never by default. Add wildflower honey or smoked maple if you like a little sweetness.
Can I order ahead?
Add items to your tray with the + buttons, then call us on +1 713 364-5155 and we will have it ready, or show the tray at the counter.
Do you have decaf?
Yes. Swiss Water decaf for any espresso drink, at no extra cost.
