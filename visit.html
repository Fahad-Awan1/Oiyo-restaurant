Good to know

Before you arrive

Still wondering about something? Dew, our chat host, knows the answers too.

Do you take walk-ins?
Always. About half the room is kept for walk-ins. Weekend mornings get busy, so booking helps if you have your heart set on a time.
Is there parking?
Free two-hour street parking on Willow Lane, a bike rack by the door, and the #15 bus stops a block away.
Can I bring my dog?
Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the terrace, and there’s always water by the door.
Is Oiyo accessible?
Yes. The café and garden room are step-free, with an accessible restroom. Mention it when booking and we’ll save a spacious table.
Is there Wi-Fi?
Yes, and the window bar has sockets. On busy weekend mornings we gently ask laptop guests to share the space.
