Seven slow years

A little more, every season.

2019

A cart and a kettle

Two friends, one copper kettle and a cart at the Saturday market. We sold out of pour-overs by ten.

2020

The starter we shared

In a quiet year we baked for our street and handed out jars of sourdough starter. Its grandchildren still bake every loaf.

2021

A room on Willow Lane

Eighteen seats, one window bar and a promise: no one is ever rushed out.

2023

Roasting, together

We began roasting with a small partner across the river, three mornings a week, twelve kilos at a time.

2024

The garden room

We opened onto the courtyard: plants, long tables and room for gatherings and Garden Suppers.

2026

Rituals, taught

Latte Art Saturdays, matcha ceremonies and home brewing classes. The ritual travels home with you.

Meet the Makers

We’re a small team of dreamers, doers and detail-lovers, united by a passion for ritual and community. Bakers who start at four, baristas who remember your order, and a chef who forages on weekends.
